THE RUNTIME LOOP

Observe. Understand. Govern.
Execute. Judge. Learn.

Six stages, kept distinct on purpose. Collapsing them is how organizations lose track of what their AI actually did.

01

Observe

A connected system produces an observation. A Signal is what was observed — not an interpretation of it, and not an instruction.

Signal
02

Understand

Nieme resolves what the observation relates to and what institutional context applies. Observation is not authority.

Context / State
03

Govern

Authority and policy decide what may happen. Suggested work is not authorized execution — proposing and permitting are separate acts.

Operation
04

Execute

Authorized work runs — through Nieme's own capabilities, a model, an agent, an external system, or a person.

Execution / Evidence
05

Judge

A person evaluates what was produced against the record supporting it. Review and Decision are governed acts, not generic feedback.

Review / Decision
06

Learn

An accepted outcome can change the context future work starts from — only by becoming durable state, never by implication.

Updated state

THE DURABLE IDENTITY OF CONSEQUENTIAL WORK

An Operation is more than a prompt.

When work is consequential, it gets a durable identity that holds its parts together — so people manage an outcome instead of a chain of prompts.

ObjectiveWhat this work is for.
ContextThe institutional material it may draw on.
AuthorityWhat is permitted, and who decides.
Execution stateWhat has actually happened so far.
EvidenceThe record supporting what was produced.
ReviewThe human judgment applied to it.
OutcomeWhat was accepted, and what follows from it.

An Operation is not the whole system. Signals, Sources, Decisions, Policies, Evidence and durable institutional state exist in their own right — an Operation is how consequential work gets an identity.

RESULT, EVIDENCE, DECISION

Execution is not the end of the work.

RESULT

What execution produced.

EVIDENCE

The supporting record used to evaluate what happened.

DECISION

What a person accepts, sends back, or rejects.

A result is not evidence, and neither one is a decision. Keeping them apart is what lets someone say, later, why this was accepted.

ORGANIZATIONS LEARN

What happened changes what Nieme knows next time.

Accepted outcomes, supporting Evidence, Decisions and durable observations can strengthen the institutional state used for future work. Not everything becomes knowledge — what a person accepts is what carries forward.

Accepted outcome → Durable state → Stronger context next time

Organizations don't become smarter because they have better AI. They become smarter because what they decided is still there the next time it matters.
