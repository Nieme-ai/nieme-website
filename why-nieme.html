WHY NIEME

AI can produce more work. Your organization still has to direct it.

Nieme is the control plane for AI work. It observes the systems you already use, turns requests and meaningful changes into governed work, keeps people in charge of consequential decisions, and carries Evidence, decisions and accepted outcomes forward.

WHAT NIEME DOES NOT TAKE

Three things you never have to hand over.

01WORK CAN STAY DISTRIBUTED

Keep the work where it works.

Nieme does not ask your company to move its work into Nieme in order for Nieme to govern it. Developers stay in GitHub. Documents stay in Drive or OneDrive. Teams stay in Slack. Agents stay in their own runtimes.

What Nieme maintains is the continuity across them — the context, the authority, the Evidence and the outcomes. A migration is a price most companies never get to pay back. This asks for none.

02AUTHORITY CAN STAY EXPLICIT

Delegate the work without delegating the decision.

Nieme separates proposing work, authorizing it, executing it, and accepting the result. Authority is stated before the work runs, not reconstructed afterwards.

And the record travels with the outcome: approved intent, the sources it drew on, the execution, the Evidence and the Decision stay connected to the operation they belong to. A leader can review the result with its supporting record instead of a summary detached from how the work was done.

03LEARNING CAN STAY WITH THE COMPANY

Make completed work useful to the next team.

Accepted outcomes, their Evidence, Decisions and durable observations can strengthen the institutional state the next piece of work starts from. People decide what carries forward — learning becomes an organizational asset instead of a private memory.
